# Completed location artwork

`bierpaleis.png` is generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using
`public/rewards/bierpaleis.png` as the style reference. It fills hexagon 1 after
the location quiz is completed, regardless of whether the answer was correct.
The app applies the hexagon crop and completion badge.

## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a new square map-tile illustration using the supplied Bierpaleis artwork as a style and architectural reference. Subject: Het Bierpaleis, a spectacular golden beer palace with barrel-shaped side towers, a grand glowing open entrance, a large beer goblet crest and crowned rat gargoyles. Psychedelic LSD poster storybook style: bold black ink contours, luminous gold, electric turquoise, hot magenta, violet and orange swirling sky. Composition optimized for a tiny point-up hexagon map tile: entire recognizable palace silhouette centered inside the middle 65% of the square, roof apex high center, broad barrel towers at mid-height, entrance at low center, no important detail in square corners. Make the palace large, readable and celebratory with simplified bold masses, not a distant landscape. Full bleed painted background, no actual hexagon frame (the app clips it), no text, no numbers, no watermark. Keep the reference's fantasy Antwerp architectural character and rich painted texture.
